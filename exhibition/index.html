Mindlessness

Slowing Down Life through Net Art

Curated by Katie Cho

About the Exhibition

These three pieces are examples of net art that have no clearly defined purpose other than to provide a satisfying space of comfort where users can mindlessly play with colors and visual effects without any deeper meaning. All created in the late 2000s and early 2010s, these works emerged during a time when the internet was becoming increasingly complex and interconnected. People were logging onto their computers with specific goals in mind: tasks needed to be completed, emails needed to be written, and assignments needed to be submitted.

I think these artists created their websites as a way to offer an escape from the increasingly busy online world. Their simplistic yet clean and effective designs draw users in, allowing them to spend a few minutes exploring or becoming entranced by a seemingly "meaningless" website. Rather than demanding productivity or attention for a specific purpose, these works encourage users to simply exist in the moment. In this sense, these websites serve as a haven of sorts, a shared digital space where people can relax, slow down, and briefly step away from the never-ending tasks and responsibilities of an increasingly fast-paced world.

01. This Is Not a Place

Michael Manning | 2013

This interactive website displays a collection of screens, including phones and computers. As users move their mouse across the screens, satisfying water-like ripples radiate outward, creating a calming visual experience.

02. Bouncy Balls

Paul Neave | 2008

A playful screen filled with colorful bouncing balls of different sizes. Users can click and drag the balls, change the screen's appearance, adjust the number of balls, and experiment with sound effects.

03. Falling Falling

Rafaël Rozendaal | 2011

A mesmerizing webpage where cascading layers of color continuously fall away to reveal new colors underneath. Its repetitive motion creates a hypnotic and satisfying visual experience that encourages viewers to simply watch and enjoy.
